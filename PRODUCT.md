## Platform

web

## Users
Product, support and marketing teams at companies with a lot of customer feedback (reviews, emails, surveys, social posts). They want to know what customers feel, what's getting worse, and what to fix first.

## Product Purpose
InsightFlow AI aggregates feedback from many channels, classifies sentiment (including sarcasm and mixed signals), and shows it in an analytics workspace (mood score, weekly volume, trends), AI recommendations with priorities, a reports centre, and settings/subscription. A marketing landing page sells it.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single `insightflow-ai.html` (inline CSS/JS) with Chart.js from cdnjs; FastAPI backend with JWT.

## Brand Commitments
- Name "InsightFlow AI".

## Evidence on Hand
- Claims in copy (98.2% accuracy, 40+ languages, 12+ sources) are product marketing to keep but not extend. Dashboard numbers are demo data.

## Product Principles
1. Feelings as data, but readable by anyone in the meeting.
2. Always end with what to do next.
3. Precise and calm; no hype words.
